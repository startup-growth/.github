# Startup Growth

**Practical resources for startup growth, growth marketing, and customer acquisition.**

Startup Growth creates reference materials for founders, marketers, and small teams figuring out how to grow a business.

The focus is on work you can actually use: understanding growth metrics, choosing acquisition channels, improving conversion and retention, and testing whether a strategy is working. We explain the terminology, but also what it means when you're making decisions.

## Projects

### [Growth Marketing Glossary](https://startup-growth.github.io/growth-marketing-glossary/)

A growing collection of practical guides to growth marketing concepts, with definitions, examples, calculations, and links between related topics.

The first 25 entries cover growth loops, product-market fit, customer acquisition cost (CAC), customer lifetime value (LTV), search engine optimization (SEO), performance marketing, lifecycle marketing, A/B testing, cohort analysis, and more.
